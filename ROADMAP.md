## Nivel 3 — Patrones de diseño (GoF)

Primero los más usados en aplicaciones empresariales Java; el resto después.

**Primera vuelta:** Strategy, Factory Method, Builder, Adapter, Facade, Decorator, Proxy, Observer, Template Method, Chain of Responsibility, Singleton.

**Segunda vuelta:** Abstract Factory, Prototype, Composite, Bridge, Flyweight, Command, State, Iterator, Mediator, Memento, Visitor, Interpreter.

| Familia | Patrones |
|---|---|
| Creacionales | Factory Method, Abstract Factory, Builder, Singleton, Prototype |
| Estructurales | Adapter, Facade, Decorator, Proxy, Composite, Bridge, Flyweight |
| Comportamiento | Strategy, Observer, Command, Template Method, State, Chain of Responsibility, Iterator, Mediator, Memento, Visitor, Interpreter |
